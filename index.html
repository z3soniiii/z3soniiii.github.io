About Jatin Real Music

Jatin Real Music is the artist project of Indian singer-songwriter Jatin Soni, also known as Z3 Soni. From Haryana, he writes, composes, produces and performs his own original Hindi songs — building a catalogue that speaks in the language of everyday North India.

His music moves between heartfelt ballads and celebratory festival songs: the heartbreak of "Tumhare Baad Koi Nahin", the devotion of "Meri Har Dua Mein", the memory of "Woh Din" and his latest single "Woh Shaam Bhi Baaki Hai". His rule for every release is simple — original melody, original words, honest emotion. No covers, no copied tunes, no shortcuts.
